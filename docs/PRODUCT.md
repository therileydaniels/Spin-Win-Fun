# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Creators, streamers, teachers, team leads, and event hosts who need a fast, fair way to pick a winner or make a decision in front of an audience. They reach for QuickWheel mid-task — on stream, in a classroom, in a meeting, at an event — often on mobile or as an OBS browser source. The job to be done: *build a wheel, spin it, and reveal a winner with a moment of drama*, then optionally save/share/reuse it. Free users get the core loop; Pro users unlock higher caps and presentation/branding polish.

## Product Purpose
QuickWheel is a prize-wheel spinner PWA: customize segments, spin to pick a winner, save/load wheels, and share them by URL or embed them in OBS. Success is the spin feeling *fair, fast, and satisfying* — a clean build experience that gets out of the way, and a reveal that lands with a small hit of delight. The product earns its keep when someone reaches for it repeatedly because it's the most pleasant tool for the job.

## Brand Personality
**Luxe, playful, confident.** Premium and polished but never stuffy — there's a wink of fun in the spin and the reveal. Voice is warm and direct, not corporate. The interface should feel like a well-made object: considered spacing, real depth where it counts, motion that rewards rather than decorates. Delight is earned through craft, not gimmicks.

## Anti-references
- **Cheap/spammy giveaway tools** — garish casino vibes, ad-stuffed "spin to win" pages, fake-prize energy. QuickWheel is the opposite: trustworthy and tasteful.
- **Generic SaaS dashboard** — sterile cookie-cutter admin template, cards-everywhere, zero personality.
- **Childish / clip-art** — Comic Sans, primary-color clip art, kids'-toy aesthetic. Playful ≠ juvenile.
- **Flat & lifeless** — a static form that happens to spin. Depth and motion are part of the product, not afterthoughts.

## Design Principles
1. **The wheel is the hero.** Every screen orbits the spin-and-reveal moment; chrome stays quiet so the wheel carries the stage. Nothing competes with it for attention.
2. **Delight is earned through craft.** Motion, depth, and color reward the user at meaningful moments (the spin, the win) — never decoration for its own sake. If an effect doesn't make the moment land harder, cut it.
3. **Premium means restraint, not more.** Luxe comes from precise spacing, honest hierarchy, and confident typography — not from piling on gradients, shadows, and glass. Spend flourish where it pays off.
4. **Fast and fair, always.** The build-to-spin path is short and obvious; the tool gets out of the way. Performance and responsiveness are features.
5. **One system, every surface.** Home, my-wheels, templates, pricing, and embed share the same tokens, spacing rhythm, and components. Consistency is what separates "polished product" from "page that drifted."

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target **WCAG 2.1 AA**. Body text ≥4.5:1 against its background (watch muted-gray-on-dark drift); large/bold text ≥3:1; visible focus states on all interactive elements. The spin animation and other motion must honor `prefers-reduced-motion` with a non-animated fallback. Because the wheel is color-heavy, winner indication should not rely on color alone (pointer position + label/highlight carry it). Touch targets ≥48px on mobile.
