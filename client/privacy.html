Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 22, 2026

QuickWheel ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use quickwheel.co (the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

We do NOT collect personal information directly. QuickWheel does not require accounts, signups, or registration.

Automatically Collected Information

When you use QuickWheel, we may automatically collect:

  • Usage Analytics: Via Google Analytics, we collect anonymized data about how visitors use the Service (pages viewed, time spent, general location)
  • Server Logs: Our hosting provider (Railway) may log IP addresses, browser type, and access times for security and performance purposes

Local Storage

QuickWheel stores data locally in your browser using localStorage, including:

  • Your custom wheels and settings
  • Visit timestamps (for returning user redirect logic)
  • Presentation mode preferences

This data never leaves your device. It is stored locally on your computer and is not transmitted to our servers.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Improve and optimize the Service
  • Understand how users interact with QuickWheel
  • Monitor for security issues and abuse
  • Analyze traffic patterns and usage trends

3. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use QuickWheel. Google Analytics collects information anonymously and reports website trends without identifying individual visitors.

Google Analytics may use cookies. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy

4. Cookies and Tracking

QuickWheel uses minimal cookies:

  • Google Analytics cookies: For anonymous usage tracking (can be blocked via browser settings)
  • No advertising cookies: We do not use cookies for targeted advertising

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. This may affect Google Analytics tracking but will not impact QuickWheel's core functionality.

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do NOT sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • Google Analytics: Anonymized usage data as described above
  • Railway (Hosting Provider): Server logs and IP addresses for hosting and security purposes
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, court order, or government request

6. Data Retention

  • localStorage data: Stored indefinitely on your device until you clear it manually
  • Server logs: Retained by Railway per their data retention policy
  • Google Analytics data: Retained per Google's default settings (26 months)

7. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Clear localStorage: Delete your browser's localStorage at any time to remove all locally saved wheels and preferences
  • Opt out of Google Analytics: Use the Google Analytics Opt-out Add-on or browser settings
  • Disable cookies: Configure your browser to block or delete cookies

8. Children's Privacy

QuickWheel is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a minor, contact us immediately at BookRD@protonmail.com.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. International Users

QuickWheel is operated from the United States. If you access the Service from outside the U.S., your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the U.S.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will be effective immediately upon posting. Your continued use of QuickWheel constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
